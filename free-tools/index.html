Free SEO checker

Our free SEO checker tests a live web page in about 20 seconds. It looks at the title, description, headings, indexing, schema, mobile setup, HTTPS, robots.txt and sitemap, then gives a plain-English fix for each problem. There is no sign-up, and the tool reads the page once the way a crawler would.

It will not promise rankings, and it will not replace a full audit. It is a fast way to see whether the basics are in place before you spend time on content or design.
