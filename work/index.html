NextGen Fusion is a web development team with offices in Lucknow and Mumbai. With 100+ projects delivered, the public work is the most useful way to see how we build. The full index lives on our main site at nextgenfusion.in/work. A few of the write-ups are below.

TerrestrialYT

TerrestrialYT is a WooCommerce store for gaming and anime merchandise. The build covers product variations, Discord integration and global shipping — the kind of catalogue work a creator store needs once merch leaves a single product page.

DeeToo

DeeToo is a WooCommerce shop for mobile accessories, organised by brand, series and exact model rather than a generic “fits most” list. The useful part of the work is the catalogue structure: a customer should be able to find a case or charger for the phone they actually own.

Cleanship

Cleanship is a Next.js site for a marine cleaning company. The site uses landing pages for the services and ports the company covers, so a visitor looking for a specific location does not have to dig through a generic brochure page.
